# Client Brief

## Appointment Booking System

We are a service-based business that accepts appointments from customers.

At the moment, appointments are managed through phone calls, messages and other communication channels.

As the number of customers grows, managing appointments manually is becoming increasingly difficult.

We would like to explore a digital solution that makes it easier for customers to arrange appointments and for the business to manage its schedule.

The system should help us manage our appointments more effectively and reduce problems caused by manual scheduling.

We are open to your team's recommendations on how the system should work.
